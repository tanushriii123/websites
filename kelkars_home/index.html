GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll discuss it with you.

What information should I bring to the first meeting?

Share the site location and size if known, your needs, approximate budget, preferred timeline, and any reference images or existing plans. It's okay if you don't have everything yet.

How are design fees calculated?

Fees depend on project type, size, complexity, deliverables, and the level of site or coordination support required. A written proposal should clarify fees, scope, payment milestones, and exclusions.

Can you help with an existing home or renovation?

Renovation and interior projects can be discussed. Feasibility depends on the site, scope, structural considerations, and the services available for your project.

Do you provide construction and approval services?

These requirements vary by project and location. Confirm the exact services, consultant coordination, statutory approvals, and site responsibilities directly with the studio before appointment.

How do I get started?

Complete the enquiry form below with your contact details and a short description. The form in this starter website demonstrates the front-end experience; it needs a backend or form service to deliver enquiries to the studio.
